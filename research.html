What we study

Research

Technology, training and safety across the whole lifespan.

Core Focus Areas

Four areas anchor the lab's work with people with IDD and their supporters.

Assistive and Accessible Technology

Matching tools such as communication apps and AAC to each person's needs and abilities.

Cybersecurity Digital Literacy

Helping individuals with IDD and their supporters protect personal information and avoid scams.

Ethical AI

Responsible, transparent, and person-centered use of AI in support, education, and care.

Emerging Technology

Evaluating tools such as VR/AR, smart glasses, and wearables for people with IDD.

Research Focus

Current research questions the lab is pursuing.

Technology Training for Supporters

Develop practical training for direct support professionals, teachers, families, and other staff who support individuals with IDD, and study how they transfer what they learn to the people they support.

Safe Technology Use Across the Lifespan

Design digital literacy and cybersecurity guidance suited to each life stage, from children to older adults with IDD.

Ethical AI in Disability Services

Identify guidelines for privacy, consent, and bias when AI is used with people with IDD.
