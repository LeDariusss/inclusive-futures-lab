Research Areas

Research

Exploring technology, accessibility, and online safety for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

Main Areas of Research

The Inclusive Futures Lab studies how technology can be used to improve accessibility, communication, and everyday life for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

Assistive and Accessible Technology

Researching technology tools that help people communicate, learn, and complete everyday tasks more independently.

Cybersecurity and Digital Literacy

Studying ways to help individuals and their support networks stay safe online, protect personal information, and recognize scams.

Ethical Artificial Intelligence

Examining how artificial intelligence can be used responsibly while respecting privacy, fairness, and accessibility.

Emerging Technologies

Exploring newer technologies such as virtual reality, augmented reality, wearable devices, and smart technology.

Current Research Interests

The lab is interested in understanding how people learn and use technology in different environments and stages of life.

Technology Training

Developing and evaluating training programs that help support professionals, teachers, and families use technology more effectively.

Safe Technology Use

Investigating ways to promote digital literacy, cybersecurity awareness, and safe technology practices for individuals with disabilities.

AI and Disability Services

Examining how artificial intelligence can be introduced into disability services while addressing concerns related to privacy, consent, and responsible use.
