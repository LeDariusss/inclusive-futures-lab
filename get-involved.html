Collaboration Opportunities

University at Albany Research Labs

The Inclusive Futures Lab is interested in working with other research groups on projects related to technology, accessibility, artificial intelligence, and disability support.

  • Create and evaluate training materials that help staff, teachers, and families use technology more effectively.
  • Explore how assistive and accessible technologies can support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities.
  • Study how technology training can be applied in everyday situations.
  • Research cybersecurity, privacy, and online safety topics that affect people with disabilities.

Community Organizations and Schools

Schools and disability service organizations can work with the lab to test training programs and technology designed to support the people they serve.

Students

Undergraduate and graduate students interested in accessibility, assistive technology, cybersecurity, or artificial intelligence are encouraged to participate. Research experience is not required, making this a good opportunity to gain practical experience working on real projects.

Potential Impact on Education

  • Teach safe online habits such as protecting passwords, personal information, and recognizing online scams.
  • Increase awareness of accessible and assistive technologies that can improve communication and independence.
  • Help future educators and support professionals learn how to use technology and AI responsibly.
  • Provide training resources that can support staff, families, and individuals throughout different stages of life.
  • Strengthen partnerships between colleges, schools, and organizations that support people with disabilities.
