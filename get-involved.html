Collaboration (CEHC and Partners)

UAlbany Research Labs

The lab welcomes collaborations to:

  • Develop and test technology training for direct support professionals, teachers, and families.
  • Evaluate assistive and accessible technologies with people with IDD.
  • Study how supporters transfer training into everyday practice.
  • Examine privacy, consent, and cybersecurity for people with IDD.

Disability Service Organizations and Schools

Pilot training and technology with the people they support and their staff.

Students

The lab is open to undergraduate and graduate students interested in assistive technology, accessibility, cybersecurity, and ethical AI. No prior research experience is required.

Contact the lab

Potential Impact on Education

  • Teach safe habits such as password and personal information security and scam spotting.
  • Raise awareness of accessible and assistive technologies, including communication apps.
  • Prepare future support professionals and educators to use technology and AI responsibly.
  • Build training that works for staff and families at every stage of a person's life.
  • Foster partnerships between higher education and organizations that serve people with IDD.
