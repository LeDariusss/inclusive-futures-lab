People

Faculty and students involved with the Inclusive Futures Lab.

Lab Members

The Inclusive Futures Lab includes faculty leadership and student researchers who share an interest in accessibility, cybersecurity, assistive technology, and artificial intelligence.

CJ

Crystal Jones-Howe

Lab Director and Adjunct Instructor, Department of Cybersecurity, University at Albany

Crystal Jones-Howe leads the lab's research efforts and focuses on topics related to accessibility, technology training, cybersecurity awareness, and ethical technology use for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities.

MC

Ph.D. Student, Informatics, University at Albany

Student Involvement

The lab encourages both undergraduate and graduate students to get involved in research and technology-focused projects. Students can gain experience while learning about accessibility, cybersecurity, and ethical uses of technology.

Working with faculty and other students provides opportunities to develop research skills, collaborate on projects, and learn about challenges that affect individuals with disabilities and their support networks.

This webpage was created as part of a college web development project. The purpose of the page is to provide information about the people involved in the Inclusive Futures Lab while demonstrating web design and development skills.
