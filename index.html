Inclusive Futures Lab

Exploring how technology can be used safely and responsibly to support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities and the people who assist them.

Our Mission

The Inclusive Futures Lab focuses on researching how technology, assistive tools, cybersecurity practices, and artificial intelligence can improve accessibility and quality of life for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD).

The lab also examines ways that families, educators, direct support professionals, and service providers can safely use technology in everyday settings. A major goal is to promote technology awareness, accessibility, and online safety for individuals with different support needs throughout their lives.

Areas of Focus

Research and technology support can have an impact throughout many stages of life:

  • Early childhood and intervention services
  • K-12 education and learning support
  • Transitioning into adulthood
  • Employment and workplace participation
  • Independent living and aging services

Why This Research Matters

Access to technology continues to grow, but many individuals with IDD and their support networks may not receive enough training on how to safely and effectively use new tools and online resources.

Topics such as cybersecurity, privacy protection, accessible design, and artificial intelligence are becoming increasingly important. Through research and education, the Inclusive Futures Lab aims to help promote safe technology use while encouraging independence, communication, and participation in daily life.
