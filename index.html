Inclusive Futures Lab

Safe, ethical technology for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities and the people who support them.

Mission

The Inclusive Futures Lab researches how assistive and emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence (AI), can safely and ethically support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) and the people who support them, from birth to death.

The lab develops technology training, cybersecurity awareness, and accessible technology practices for individuals with IDD, their families, teachers, direct support professionals, clinicians, and service providers.

Foundational Work

The lab connects disability services research with emerging technology across the whole lifespan:

  • Early intervention
  • School years
  • The transition to adulthood
  • Employment and day services
  • Aging
See our research

Why It Matters

People with IDD and the staff and families who support them often have the least access to technology training, yet face real risks such as scams, loss of privacy, and misuse of AI.

The Inclusive Futures Lab builds practical, accessible training so technology can improve independence, communication, and quality of life from birth to death.
